Our approach

Five steps from skill gap to measured impact

  • Your first workshop live in about four weeks
  • Follow-up after every module, because that is where behaviour changes

  1. Diagnose

    Weeks 1–2

    Interviews, surveys and skill assessments produce a skill-gap report and agreed success metrics.

  2. Design

    Weeks 3–4

    A learning roadmap with content built around your own cases and processes.

  3. Deliver

    Months 2–6

    Workshops, simulations, role-plays and action learning, on-site or virtual.

  4. Reinforce

    30–90 days after

    Coaching, manager toolkits, short refreshers and applied projects.

  5. Measure

    Months 3–6

    An impact report against the baseline, with recommendations for what comes next.

Our story

Why we started

Most of us have sat through a training day that felt good and changed nothing. The slides were sharp, the energy was high, and by the following Monday everyone was back to the old way of working.

Our founder, Suneeth Nag S N, has spent more than 21 years developing people, processes and customer experience across industries, and saw this pattern again and again. The problem was rarely the people. It was training designed as an event instead of a journey, with no link between people and business goals, no practice on the job and no check on skills.

ElearnersForum was founded in 2020 to fix that. We start every program with the business problem, build it around the participants' real work, and stay with them until new habits stick.

What our name means

  • Elearners: Every employee, from a fresh graduate to a CEO, is a lifelong learner
  • Forum: People learn best together, sharing experience, practising and getting honest feedback

 Knowledge is the seed to a Better Life.

What drives us

Mission, Vision and Values

Our Mission

To help organisations grow through confident people, strong leaders and well-run processes, with training that changes how work gets done.

Our Vision

Workplaces where every person keeps learning, every manager is a coach, and every process keeps getting better.

Our Values

  • Practical: every idea tested on real work
  • Personal: programs fit your people and culture
  • Honest: results measured and reported plainly
  • Lasting: we stay until new habits stick

What makes us different

Five principles behind every program

Business first

Every program starts from a business problem you name, not from a course catalogue.

Learning by doing

Role-plays, simulations, case studies and real projects. Lecture stays under 30% of session time.

Built for you

Examples, cases and language from your industry, your culture and your own processes.

We stay after the workshop

Coaching, follow-up nudges and on-the-job projects for 30–90 days after each module.

Measured on four levels

Reaction, learning, behaviour and business results, reported in writing for every program.

How we gather feedback

Every program is reviewed twice

At the end of each session

  • Relevance, facilitator rating and one action each participant will take
  • Shared with the client's HR team within a week

60–90 days later

  • Participants and their managers report what changed at work
  • Results go into the program's impact report

Privacy

How we use your details

  • We collect only what you send us
  • We never sell or rent your details

What we collect

When you fill in a form on this website (a training enquiry, a meeting request or a trainer application), we receive what you type: your name, email, mobile number and, where you give them, your company, designation, city and training needs. If you email or WhatsApp us, we receive your message and contact details.

Why we use it

To reply to you, arrange discovery calls and meetings, prepare training proposals, run the programs you book and review trainer applications. We do not use your details for anything else without asking you first.

How your details reach us

Our forms send your answers to our email inbox through FormSubmit, an email forwarding service, which also emails you a copy. If you choose to send your details on WhatsApp, they go through WhatsApp.

Who we share them with

We do not sell or rent your details. We share them only when needed to deliver your program, for example with the facilitator assigned to it, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep them

We keep your details while we respond to you and run any program you book, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Visitor statistics

We may use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. It uses cookies and does not tell us who you are.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the details we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Email us at info@elearnersforum.com.
